Our Story

About Step Leatherworks Limited

Founded in Seeta, Mukono in 2014 with one realization: most leather products in Uganda were imported — and most Ugandans had no chance to learn the craft.

Mission

A skilled workforce produces high quality products.

Step Leatherworks Limited continuously seeks to produce a skilled labor force in leather production through training and mentorship — while making quality, stylish leather products, from shoes to bags, wallets and belts.

Since 2014, the organization has trained thousands of youth throughout Uganda — most especially in Karamoja, Northern and Central Uganda — with one thing always in mind: trainees must be able to produce quality leather products at the end of the training.
