Skilling Communities

Capacity Building

We take leather-production skills to the communities that need them most — youth, women and teenage mothers across Karamoja, Northern and Central Uganda — in partnership with national and international organizations.

What we deliver

Technical skilling, business skills & mentorship

  • Technical training in designing and making shoes, bags, wallets and belts
  • Shoe-making tools and machinery training
  • Business and life skills, marketing and record keeping
  • Counseling and guidance for vulnerable youth
  • Mentorship as an integral part of every program
  • Consultancy in leather production for organizations and individuals

Programs delivered since 2014

Trainings held with our partners

2021 · Vision for Africa International

Certificate training in leather production — factory and store operations, marketing, and understanding the market.

2021 · Welthunger International

Youth and women's groups in Seeta, Mukono — designing and making open shoes, using tools and machinery, marketing, and store management.

2020 · USSIA — Uganda Small Scale Industries Association

Three-month training in Seeta, Mukono — shoes, bags, wallets and belts, machinery, marketing and business management.

2019 · Welthunger International

Three-month training in Seeta, Mukono — full leather production skills for income generation.

Save the Children — Amuru District

Four-month project mobilizing out-of-school youth — counseling and guidance, shoe, wallet and belt making, business records.

Save the Children — Nwoya District

Skilling for teenage mothers who had dropped out of school — leather crafts, counseling, and business management.

2016 · Swiss Contact

Youth training in Gayaza, Wakiso — designing and making shoes, wallets and belts, plus marketing and record keeping.

2015 · Defense for Children International

Training for young people in Oyam District, Loro Sub-County — open shoes, wallets, belts and business skills.
