Certified Skills

Formal Training — UVTAB Curriculum

Structured, certified training in leather production based on the UVTAB curriculum, with a mentorship program built in. Our goal is simple: every trainee finishes able to produce quality, market-ready leather products.

The program

Learn the trade, from design to business

  • Designing and making shoes, bags, wallets and belts
  • Using shoe-making tools and machinery
  • Managing the operations of a leather production factory
  • Managing a leather products store
  • Marketing leather products and understanding the market
  • Business and life skills, including record keeping

Training runs in structured intakes at our Seeta, Mukono workshop. Contact us for the next intake dates, fees and enrollment requirements.

Mentorship

Training doesn't end at graduation

A mentorship program is an integral part of every training we run. Graduates are followed up and guided as they start their own production — because a skilled workforce only matters if it produces, earns, and grows.
